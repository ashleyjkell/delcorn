# Delcorn — background brief

Delcorn Learning Ltd is a **fictional** organisation created for AI and Copilot training. It is modelled on the
shape of a leading UK independent training provider (ITP) so that learners recognise the types of work, data and
documents they deal with every day. All people, employers, figures and records are invented.

## What Delcorn does
- Delivers **levy-funded apprenticeships** (Levels 3–7) and **commercial programmes** to large UK employers.
- Organised into five academies: **AI**, **Data**, **Digital**, **Leadership** and **People**.
- Delivery is **fully remote**: skills coaches hold 1:1 coaching and 12-weekly tripartite progress reviews, plus live
  online workshops; learners log evidence and off-the-job (OTJ) hours in the **Aptiva** e-portfolio.
- Employer partners span financial services, retail, NHS/healthcare, utilities, public sector, technology,
  logistics and charities. The largest are Northgrid Energy, Valemont Insurance, Brightwell Foods and Halden Valley
  NHS Foundation Trust.

## Size and shape (as at 5 November 2026)
- ~142 staff, of whom ~60 are skills coaches; HQ in London with co-working hubs in Leeds and Manchester.
- ~2,300 apprentices enrolled since August 2023; ~1,400 currently on programme.
- Revenue c. £12m in FY2025/26 (year end 31 July), mostly apprenticeship funding plus ~£2.1m commercial income.
- Owned since March 2025 by Meridian Education Group (fictional), operating independently.

## Regulation and frameworks
- **Ofsted** inspects quality under the education inspection framework; Delcorn's February 2026 inspection rated it
  "Strong standard" across all areas.
- **Department for Education** funding rules (formerly ESFA): eligibility, OTJ training, the monthly **ILR** return,
  funding audits. **Skills England** sets apprenticeship standards and units.
- Independent **End-Point Assessment Organisations** (EPAOs) grade apprentices at the end of their programmes.
- **Safeguarding** and the **Prevent duty**, **UK GDPR**, and accessibility duties.

## Systems
Aptiva (e-portfolio, fictional), PipelineHQ (CRM, fictional), Ledgerline (finance, fictional), PeopleFlow (HR,
fictional), FixDesk (service desk, fictional), Microsoft 365 with Copilot, Power BI.

## Current themes (autumn 2026)
- Rolling out **Microsoft 365 Copilot** to all staff, while tightening SharePoint permissions and sensitivity labels.
- Launch of the **Level 4 AI & Automation Practitioner** programme and new **apprenticeship units** under the growth
  and skills levy.
- Funding policy changes (e.g. reduced Level 7 eligibility) put pressure on Senior Leader and Senior People
  Professional enrolments.
- Coach caseloads, overdue progress reviews and withdrawals are the main operational focus.
- Integration with Meridian group finance and HR processes.

supplier register, staff directory, IT tickets; plus policies, SAR/QIP, board packs, proposals, contracts, workshop
