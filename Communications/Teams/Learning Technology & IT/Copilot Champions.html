Fatima Hussain 26/10/2026 09:40

My go-to for review summaries: paste in your rough notes and ask: "Draft a progress review summary for a Level 4 Data Analyst apprentice using these notes. Use UK English, three short paragraphs: progress against KSBs, OTJ position, and SMART targets for the next 12 weeks. Don't add anything that isn't in my notes." That last line stops it inventing things. Then I edit it into my own words before signing in Aptiva.
